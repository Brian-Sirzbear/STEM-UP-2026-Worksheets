# Thoughts on the Design of the Data Science Program
Progress has been less than expected due at least partially to apprehensions and indecisions over how we are to structure the Program.
* What is the relationship between the Labs, the Reviews, and the Exercises?
* We have plans to create different versions of labs to suit the differing levels of 8th-9th, 10th, and 11th-12th; How these versions will differ is still being decided, but this will likely factor into how we configure the Reviews and Exercises.
* I am refraining from putting CodeBuilder problems in the Review 1; This is because I feel that the Exercises 1 already possess an immense amount of calculation-style problems (which is a big focus in Lab 1), and the variety of possible solutions for the calculation-involved code snippets makes programming these CodeBuilder programs quite tedious.
* I am still thinking about putting CodeBuilder and other HTML applications in the Exercises, though I'm conflicted over the sheer amount of space that the CodeBuilder's interface takes up.
